# Phase 3: Design Impact

## Purpose

Determine whether the enhancement fits the current design or requires architecture discussion.

## Ask

1. Does the enhancement fit existing architecture?
2. Does it introduce new contracts, schemas, or dependencies?
3. Does it affect security, data handling, CI/CD, or generated artifacts?
4. Does it require a new pattern?

## Produce

- design impact summary
- risk classification
- required gates
- recommendation: proceed, revise, or switch workflow

## Stop / Continue

Ask whether to proceed to execution planning or switch to architecture workflow.
