# Phase 1: Enhancement Intake

## Purpose

Clarify the requested behavior and boundaries.

## Ask

1. What behavior or capability are we adding?
2. Who uses it?
3. What should change?
4. What should not change?
5. How will we know it works?

## Produce

- requested behavior
- affected user/system flow
- constraints
- non-goals
- acceptance criteria
- open questions

## Stop / Continue

Ask whether to revise intake or continue to pattern discovery.
