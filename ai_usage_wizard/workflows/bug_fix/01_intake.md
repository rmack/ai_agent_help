# Phase 1: Bug Intake

## Purpose

Clarify observed vs expected behavior.

## Ask

1. What happened?
2. What should have happened?
3. When did it start?
4. How severe is it?
5. What context, logs, or inputs are available?

## Produce

- defect summary
- observed behavior
- expected behavior
- known reproduction details
- suspected area
- open questions

## Stop / Continue

Ask whether to revise intake or continue to reproduction.
