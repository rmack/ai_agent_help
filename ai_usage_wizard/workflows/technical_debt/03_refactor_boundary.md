# Phase 3: Refactor Boundary

## Purpose

Define the smallest safe refactor boundary.

## Ask

1. What files/modules are in scope?
2. What is explicitly out of scope?
3. Is this a local cleanup or design change?
4. Does this require new patterns or docs?

## Produce

- in-scope files/modules
- out-of-scope files/modules
- behavior preservation rule
- risk classification
- stop conditions

## Stop / Continue

Ask whether to revise boundary or continue to execution plan.
