# Phase 1: Technical Debt Intake

## Purpose

Define the debt and why it matters.

## Ask

1. What area is debt?
2. Why is it a problem?
3. What pain does it cause?
4. What behavior must remain unchanged?

## Produce

- debt summary
- motivation
- behavior preservation expectations
- affected area
- open questions

## Stop / Continue

Ask whether to revise intake or continue to characterization.
