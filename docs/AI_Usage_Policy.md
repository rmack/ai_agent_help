# AI Usage Policy (Software Development)

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes requirements for the responsible and secure use of Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools in software development.

The organization enables AI to improve productivity while ensuring protection of intellectual property, data, and system integrity.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to:

- All employees, contractors, and third parties
- All software development activities
- All AI tools used for organizational work (approved or unapproved)

## 3. Core Principles

- **Human Accountability:** All outputs generated using AI are the responsibility of the developer using the tool.
- **Security First:** Sensitive data must not be exposed to AI systems.
- **Integrity of Systems:** AI must not introduce uncontrolled changes to architecture, behavior, or security.
- **Responsible Use:** AI is a tool to assist development, not replace engineering judgment.

## 4. Mandatory Requirements

### 4.1 Use of Approved Tools

- Only AI tools approved by the organization may be used for development involving organizational assets.
- Use of unapproved tools requires explicit authorization.

### 4.2 Adherence to Guidance and Standards

- Developers **must follow the approved AI Usage Guidance** when using AI tools.
- All work must comply with:
  - secure coding standards
  - architecture standards
  - development and review processes

### 4.3 Code Review and Validation

- All AI-generated or AI-assisted code must:
  - be reviewed by a qualified human
  - be understood by the individual submitting it
  - pass required testing and validation

### 4.4 Data Protection

- The following must not be shared with AI tools unless explicitly approved:
  - confidential or proprietary information
  - customer or regulated data (PII, PHI, financial data)
  - credentials, secrets, or access tokens

### 4.5 Intellectual Property and Licensing

- Developers must ensure that AI-generated outputs:
  - do not infringe on third-party intellectual property
  - comply with applicable licensing requirements

## 5. Prohibited Activities

- Submitting AI-generated code without review or understanding
- Exposing sensitive or restricted data to AI tools
- Using AI to bypass security controls or development processes
- Introducing unapproved architectural or technology changes through AI-generated outputs

## 6. Cost Management and Responsible Usage

- AI tool usage must be reasonable, necessary, and aligned with business value.
- Users are responsible for:
  - using AI tools efficiently
  - avoiding excessive or unnecessary usage
  - selecting appropriate models/tools for the task
- Teams and managers are responsible for:
  - monitoring usage within their area
  - ensuring usage aligns with allocated budgets
- The organization may:
  - monitor and report AI usage and associated costs
  - enforce usage limits or quotas
  - require approval for high-cost or large-scale usage
- Use of AI tools must not result in uncontrolled or unjustified cost increases.

## 7. Accountability

- Developers are fully accountable for all AI-assisted outputs.
- Managers are responsible for ensuring adherence to this policy within their teams.
- Violations may result in disciplinary action in accordance with organizational policy.

## 8. Governance and Compliance

- This policy is enforced in conjunction with:
  - Information Security policies
  - Data Protection policies
  - Secure Development standards
- Compliance may be monitored through:
  - code reviews
  - audit processes
  - tooling and logging (where applicable)
